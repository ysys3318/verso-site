Verso Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Verso does not collect your data. Everything you make in Verso stays on your device. Verso has no account, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Verso is a notebook app for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains, in plain language, what Verso uses on your device, why, and where it stays. "The developer" means the person who makes Verso, shown as the seller on Verso's App Store page.

What Verso uses, why, and where it stays

What Why Where it stays
Calendar (optional) To show your events on the daily planner page, and to let you open, add and edit them there with the system's own event screens. Your events stay in the Calendar app; Verso does not copy them. Verso's settings remember only which calendars you put under Work, Personal or hidden.
Face ID, Touch ID or passcode (optional) To open the notebooks you lock. iOS checks your face, fingerprint or passcode and tells Verso only whether it matched. Verso never sees or keeps them.
Photos, add only (optional) To save the pages you choose with Save to Photos. Verso can add pictures to your library. It cannot see, read or change anything in it.
The photo picker To put a photo you pick on a page. Verso sees only the photos you pick. It keeps a smaller copy in the notebook, without the photo's location or camera details.
Files To import a notebook file (.verso) or a backup you choose, and to save the PDFs, pictures and notebook files you export. Verso opens only the file you pick. What you export goes only where you save or send it.
Mail To write to support when you choose Contact Support. Your own email app sends the message. Verso sends nothing itself.
The clipboard To copy and paste ink, photos, stickers, text and pages, and to paste a picture you copied in another app. Verso reads the clipboard's contents only when you paste. What you copy is on your device's clipboard, where other apps can paste it, and Universal Clipboard can pass it to your other Apple devices if you use Handoff.

Where your notebooks are stored

Your notebooks, pages, handwriting, text boxes, photos, stickers, planner tasks and notes, folders and settings are stored only on your device, in Verso's own storage.

A notebook lock limits who can open a notebook in Verso. It is not encryption.

When an update changes how Verso stores your notebooks, Verso first keeps a copy of them on your device, in case the update cannot open them. The copy is removed two weeks later, or by Delete All Data.

What leaves your device

Verso never sends anything on its own. Something leaves your device only when you send it:

  • When you share pages or notebooks as a PDF or as pictures, copy a page, or export a notebook file or a backup, the files go only where you send or save them.
  • A notebook file (.verso) or backup is not encrypted and carries no lock: anyone who has the file can open it. Importing a file adds new notebooks on your device; nothing is uploaded.
  • When you email support, see Contact below.

Apple's own services work outside Verso, under your settings with Apple:

  • Your device's backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include Verso's data, as with any app.
  • Events you add or edit from the planner are saved in the Calendar app, which syncs them with the calendar accounts you set up there.
  • Files you save to or open from iCloud Drive are stored by iCloud.
  • If you turn on Share With App Developers (Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share crash reports with the developer. If you test Verso with TestFlight, Apple shares crash reports, usage information and the feedback you send with the developer, as TestFlight explains.

Deleting your data

  • To delete one notebook, touch and hold it in the library and choose Delete.
  • Settings ▸ Storage and Data ▸ Delete All Data removes every notebook with all its pages, handwriting, text boxes, photos, stickers, planner days and tasks, every folder, the copies kept for updates and Verso's temporary files, and resets all settings. It cannot be undone.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device.
  • Events in the Calendar app, pictures you saved to Photos, and files or backups you saved or sent elsewhere are not affected. They are yours to delete where they are.
  • You can turn off Calendar or Photos access in Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, and Face ID for Verso in Settings ▸ Face ID & Passcode ▸ Other Apps. The rest of Verso keeps working.

Children

Verso is a general notebook app, not one made for children. It collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because Verso does not collect your data, the developer holds nothing about you from the app. If you have emailed support, you can ask to see, correct or delete those emails at the address below. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Verso go to support.verso.app@gmail.com.

If you email support (for example with Settings ▸ Contact Support, which opens an email you can read and change before sending), your own email app sends it, and the developer receives only what you send: your email address, the name your email app shows, your message and anything you attach. The email Verso prepares adds only the app's version, the iOS version and the kind of device (for example "iPad16,3"); you can delete those lines. The support address is a Gmail account, so Google stores the messages. Your email is used only to answer you, is never sold or shared, and is kept only as long as needed to help you.

This website

This website sets no cookies, runs no scripts and loads nothing from other sites. It is published with GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs each visitor's IP address for security, as its privacy statement explains.
