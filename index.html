Verso Support

A notebook for Apple Pencil on iPhone and iPad.

Verso is a calm, paper-like notebook for handwriting. Write with Apple Pencil or your finger on lined, grid, dot-grid or blank pages, add text boxes, photos and stickers, and plan each day in a calendar notebook with a daily planner page and your calendar's events beside your handwriting. It is designed for iPhone Duo's two displays and works on every iPhone and iPad. Everything you make stays on your device.

What Verso does

Write

  • Eight pen types, a highlighter, eraser and lasso
  • Shapes snap clean when you hold the Pencil still
  • Text boxes, photos and stickers anywhere on a page
  • Pinch to zoom up to 4×

Plan

  • A calendar notebook gives every day a two-page spread
  • Focus task, A/B/C task list and a schedule from 12 AM to midnight
  • Forward unfinished tasks to tomorrow
  • Your Calendar events beside your notes (optional)

Make it yours

  • 32 cover colors or any color, matte, linen or leather
  • Folders, favorites and sorting
  • Lock a notebook with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode
  • Pages curl like paper, or slide

Share and keep

  • Share pages or notebooks as a PDF or as pictures
  • Save pages to Photos
  • Export a notebook file to open on another device
  • Back up all notebooks to one file

Verso supports Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, Reduce Motion and left-handed use.

Questions and answers

Which devices does Verso run on?

Every iPhone and iPad that runs iOS 27. On iPhone Duo the closed notebook waits on the outer display and opens to a two-page spread when you unfold the phone. iPad, and iPhone in landscape, show the same spread; narrow windows show one page at a time.

Do I need an Apple Pencil?

No. On iPad, Verso works with every Apple Pencil the iPad supports, and on iPhone Duo with Apple Pencil (USB-C) on both displays. On other iPhones your finger writes from the start, and anywhere you can turn on Settings ▸ Writing ▸ Draw with Finger. With it on, one finger draws and two fingers placed together turn pages and move a zoomed page.

Why does Verso ask for calendar access?

To show your events from the Calendar app on the planner page of a calendar notebook, and to let you open, add and edit them there with the system's own event screens. Access is optional and everything else works without it. Your events stay in the Calendar app; Verso does not copy them. You can change access any time in the Settings app ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Calendars.

Where are my notebooks stored?

Only on your device. Verso has no account, no servers and no network access. Your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) may include Verso's data, as with any app.

Does Verso sync between my devices?

No, Verso has no cloud sync. To move a notebook, touch and hold it in the library and choose Export Notebook File…, then send the .verso file with AirDrop or save it to Files. On the other device, open the file, or choose Import… in the library's menu. It arrives as a new notebook.

How do I back up my notebooks?

Settings ▸ Storage and Data ▸ Back Up All Notebooks makes one .verso file with every notebook. Save it somewhere safe, such as Files or a computer. Restore from Backup… in the same place adds the notebooks from a backup as new ones.

Is a locked notebook encrypted?

No. A lock asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode before a notebook opens in Verso; it is not encryption. An exported notebook file or backup is not encrypted and carries no lock, so anyone who has the file can open it. Keep those files somewhere private.

How do I delete notebooks or all my data?

To delete one notebook, touch and hold it in the library and choose Delete. To delete everything, open Settings ▸ Storage and Data ▸ Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes everything it stored. Events in the Calendar app, pictures saved to Photos and files you saved elsewhere are not affected.

Privacy

Verso does not collect your data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no network access.
